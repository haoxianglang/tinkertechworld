UV Printing

Print directly onto a surface, cured by UV light.

Learn how UV printers apply ink directly onto rigid or flexible surfaces—like acrylic, wood or metal—and cure it instantly with UV light. Explore how material, ink layers and finish affect the result.

What learning looks like

Skills with a purpose.

Learners see how a UV printer applies ink directly onto a surface such as acrylic, wood or metal and cures it instantly with UV light. Along the way, they prepare a design file for a specific material and print area, test settings on samples, and compare a finished print against the original design.

Learning outcomes

  • Prepare a design file sized and formatted for a specific surface and print area
  • Explain how UV-cured ink layers, gloss and texture affect a finished piece
  • Test print settings on sample materials before running a final print
  • Follow safety steps when operating UV printing equipment
  • Compare a finished print's colour and finish against the original design and adjust
