# Information Architecture

All routes below have a matching `/zh/` route. The header prioritizes Programs, Age Groups, FIRST LEGO League, Camps, About and Book a Trial. Footer and contextual links expose Gallery, FAQ, Contact and Join without overcrowding mobile navigation. Home is reachable from the brand and breadcrumbs.

| Coding | `/programs/coding.html` | Block coding and future Python distinction |

| 3D Design & Printing | `/programs/3d-design-printing.html` | Future design / fabrication pathway |

Existing `/store.html` remains a secondary fabrication inquiry page outside the educational acquisition funnel; the original product files and manifest remain unchanged. No payment/checkout is implied. It is excluded from sitemap and indexing.

Subject page → preselected trial or join. Age page → preselected grade inquiry or relevant subject pages. Future offerings lead to interest discussions rather than false checkout. Header/footer/home CTA always resolves to the inquiry page. Language switching retains the same content route and validated selections on inquiry pages.
