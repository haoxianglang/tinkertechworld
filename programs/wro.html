World Robot Olympiad

Three categories. One shared goal: think like an engineer.

Prepare for the World Robot Olympiad across three category formats—mission-based robots, a self-driving vehicle challenge and real-world research projects—each with its own age division, team size and judging focus.

What learning looks like

Skills with a purpose.

  • RoboMission (Grades 4–12) — teams of 2–3 build and program a LEGO-based robot to complete missions on a themed field within a time limit, judged on reliable, autonomous task completion.
  • Future Engineers (Grades 8–12) — teams of 2 design and program a self-driving vehicle to navigate a standardized track, judged on obstacle avoidance, lane following and parking.
  • Future Innovators (Grades 4–12) — teams of 3–5 research a real-world problem and prototype a robotics or technology solution, judged on creativity, critical thinking, communication and the design process.

Prepare a RoboMission-style challenge run

Build and program a robot to complete a themed mission field within a time limit, then refine it across repeated timed runs—the core loop behind most WRO categories.

A starting point that fits.

Open to learners with some LEGO Robotics or coding experience who want a competition-oriented challenge. Team size and age division follow the category chosen; which category, and any team placement, is discussed individually. This page is preparation, not event registration.
