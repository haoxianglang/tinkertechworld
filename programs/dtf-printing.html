DTF Printing

From a design file to a wearable transfer.

Learn how direct-to-film (DTF) printing turns a digital design into a durable, full-colour transfer that can be pressed onto fabric. Understand the process from file prep to finished press.

What learning looks like

Skills with a purpose.

  • Prepare artwork for print: resolution, colour and file format.
  • Understand the DTF process: printing, powder application and curing.
  • Press a transfer correctly: temperature, pressure and timing.

Illustrative project · Not a student result

Design and press a custom transfer

Design a small graphic, prepare the file for DTF printing, and press it onto a sample fabric piece. Compare results and adjust settings for a cleaner result.
