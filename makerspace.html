The learning approach

Learning that
connects the dots.

Robotics, coding and making work best when a learner can connect them. The TTW approach centres on a project, a question and a reason to try again.

Imagine & build

Start with a question. Make a plan, try an idea and connect it to something tangible.

Test & improve

Notice what works, investigate what doesn’t, and make a thoughtful change.

Explain & connect

Share the reasoning behind the project and carry those skills into the next challenge.

What can a parent look for?

Ask the learner to explain the goal, show a test, describe a problem and point to a change. These small pieces of evidence make progress more meaningful than a finished model alone.

A progression in understanding, not just a bigger kit.
