# About Tinker Tech World

## Philosophy
Tinker Tech World is built around a simple idea: learners understand technology more deeply when they use it to make, test and improve something of their own.
Tinker Tech World 源于一个简单理念：当学习者用技术制作、测试并改进自己的作品时，便能更深入地理解技术。

The learning approach: "The best part? 'I figured it out.'" A working robot/project is a beginning — understanding why it works, and what to try when it doesn't, is where deeper learning happens. Three-step framing shown on the homepage:
1. **Imagine & build** — start with a question, make a plan, try an idea, connect it to something tangible.
2. **Test & improve** — notice what works, investigate what doesn't, make a thoughtful change.
3. **Explain & connect** — share the reasoning behind the project, carry those skills into the next challenge.
构思与搭建 → 测试与改进 → 解释与连接。

## Values (Tinker / Tech / World)
- **Tinker** — Hands-on practice, explore the unknown. 动手实践，探索未知。
- **Tech** — Master technology, change the future. 掌握技术，改变未来。
- **World** — Embrace the world, connect the future. 面向世界，连接未来。

## Age groups / pathways
- **Grades 3–6** — "Explore & build." A welcoming starting point for curious builders: mechanisms, a first robot, how instructions become movement. Core pathway; programs: LEGO Robotics.
  3–6 年级："探索与搭建"，核心学习方向；课程：LEGO 机器人。
- **Grades 7–9** — "Control & integrate." Move from a build that works once to a system that works reliably: sensors, calibration, structured debugging. Core pathway; programs: LEGO Robotics, WRO, 3D Design & Printing, DTF Printing, UV Printing.
  7–9 年级："控制与整合"，核心学习方向；课程：LEGO 机器人、WRO、3D 设计与打印、DTF 转印、UV 打印。
- **High School** — "Discover. Specialize. Create." CAD design and a concise look at university-level engineering paths, building toward documented independent project work. No university credit or admission outcome is promised. Future pathway; programs: WRO, 3D Design & Printing, DTF Printing, UV Printing, Distilled Series, IELTS.
  高中："发现·深入·创造"，未来学习方向；不承诺大学学分或录取结果。
- **Adult** — "Stay curious. Keep making." Goal-led learning for adult beginners and returning makers; adult workshops are not currently scheduled. Future workshops; programs: Robotics for Adults, 3D Design & Printing, DTF Printing, UV Printing, IELTS.
  成人："保持好奇，持续创造"，未来工作坊，目前尚未排期。
