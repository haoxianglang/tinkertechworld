Distilled Series

Engineering paths, explained simply.

A concise, project-based look at computer science, computer engineering, electrical engineering and mechanical engineering—for a high schooler weighing university majors, not exam prep.

Suggested learners
High School
Format
Guided, project-based learning (Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Engineering)
Schedule & tuition
One class per week, 16 classes total, $60/class

What learning looks like

Skills with a purpose.

  • Compare how each discipline frames the same kind of problem.
  • Work through short, visual explanations of core ideas instead of a full semester of theory.
  • Try a small project in more than one discipline to see what the work actually feels like.

Illustrative project · Not a student result

Prototype one device, four perspectives

Take one simple device and look at it four ways: how a computer scientist would model its logic, a computer engineer would design its circuit and firmware, an electrical engineer would handle its power and signals, and a mechanical engineer would design its housing and moving parts.

Activities depend on the learner’s level and confirmed course.
