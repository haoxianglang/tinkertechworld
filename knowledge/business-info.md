## How inquiries work

This website is an inquiry site, not an automated booking or payment system.

- A visitor fills out an inquiry form (grade group, program interest, preferred timing, optional notes). Submitting it does **not** reserve a seat or confirm a class — TTW must follow up directly.
- No online payment is collected anywhere on the site.
- Before enrolling in anything, a family should get in writing: session length, number of classes, tuition and tax treatment, equipment inclusion, supervision/pickup arrangements, and refund or makeup-class terms.
- 提交咨询不等于预留席位；付款不在网站上完成；报名前请向 TTW 索取课时、课次、学费与税费、设备、指导/接送安排，以及退款或补课条款的书面说明。

## What's confirmed vs. not yet confirmed

- **LEGO Robotics** is TTW's core, currently-running pathway (Grades 3–6 and 7–9), with published per-class pricing (see programs.md).
- **World Robot Olympiad Prep** and **Distilled Series** now have confirmed format/tuition info (see programs.md), but are still listed as a "Future pathway · Register interest" status on their own pages.
- Every other program (3D Design & Printing, DTF Printing, UV Printing, Robotics for Adults, IELTS Preparation) is a **future pathway** — register interest, but tuition/schedule for these individually is "confirm with TTW before enrollment" except where noted in schedule.md.
- **Camps** are not open for registration; dates, ages, hours, fees and policies are all still to be confirmed.
- **FIRST LEGO League** team placement is not offered through the website — it's a consultation only; TTW's own team availability is not yet confirmed.
