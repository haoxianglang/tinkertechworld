## LEGO Robotics (`lego-robotics`)
**Grades 3–6 · Grades 7–9** — Core learning pathway, currently running (ask about availability).
中文：LEGO 机器人（3–6 年级 · 7–9 年级）— 核心学习方向，已开班（请咨询开班情况）。

Big ideas start with a first build. Connect mechanisms, motors and code. Learn to build a robot, explain what it does, and improve how reliably it works.
从第一次搭建，开始探索大想法。将机械结构、电机与代码连接起来。

**Tuition (published)**:
- Grades 3–6: one class per week, 16 classes, **$40/class**. 每周一节课，16节课，$40每节课。
- Grades 7–9: one class per week, 16 classes, **$50/class**. 每周一节课，16节课，$50每节课。
- Format: guided, project-based learning. 指导下开展项目学习。

**Grades 3–6 — what learning looks like**: A hands-on first step into robotics. Learners build sturdy LEGO structures, connect motors and sensors, and use block-based coding to bring their robot to life.
Learning outcomes: build stable structures using gears/axles/motors; use block-based coding to control movement (forward, turn, stop); read and respond to a simple sensor input; test one change at a time and explain what happened; practise teamwork during a build challenge.
Illustrative project (not a student result): "Build a delivery robot" — design a robot to carry an object to a target, compare routes, adjust the mechanism, record what improves each attempt.

**Grades 7–9 — what learning looks like**: Builds on core construction/coding skills — combining multiple sensors, writing longer sequences and loops, refining reliability through repeated testing.
Learning outcomes: combine multiple sensor inputs; write and debug longer coding sequences including loops/conditionals; calibrate a robot's response for consistency; break a complex task into testable steps; explain design decisions and propose improvements.
Illustrative project: "Program an obstacle-avoiding robot" — add a sensor to detect obstacles, program stop/turn/reroute, test in different layouts.

**Entry note**: Beginners can start with building and block coding. Learners with experience can discuss sensor-based challenges and a suitable starting level.

## World Robot Olympiad Prep (`wro`)
**Grades 7–9 · High School** — page still shows "Future pathway · Register interest" status, but format/tuition are now published.
中文：World Robot Olympiad（7–9 年级 · 高中）— 页面状态仍为"未来学习方向·登记意向"，但形式与学费已公布。

Three categories, one shared goal: think like an engineer. Prepares learners for the World Robot Olympiad across three category formats.

**Format (published)**: Pre-Team and Competition Team. Pre-Team 和 Competition Team。
**Tuition (published)**: Starts every January. Pre-Team $60/class, Competition Team $80/class.
每年1月开始，Pre-Team $60每节课，Competition Team $80每节课。

**Categories**:
- RoboMission (Grades 4–12) — teams of 2–3 build and program a LEGO-based robot to complete missions on a themed field within a time limit, judged on reliable autonomous task completion.
- Future Engineers (Grades 8–12) — teams of 2 design/program a self-driving vehicle on a standardized track, judged on obstacle avoidance, lane following, parking.
- Future Innovators (Grades 4–12) — teams of 3–5 research a real-world problem and prototype a solution, judged on creativity, critical thinking, communication, design process.

**Entry note**: Open to learners with some LEGO Robotics or coding experience wanting a competition-oriented challenge. Team size/age division follow the chosen category; this page is preparation, not event registration. Team placement and official event registration go through WRO's National Organizer, separately from TTW.

## 3D Design & Printing (`3d-design-printing`)
**Grades 7–9 · High School · Adult** — Future pathway · Register interest. Format/tuition: confirm with TTW before enrollment (not yet published as a fixed price).
中文：3D 设计与打印（7–9 年级 · 高中 · 成人）— 未来学习方向·登记意向；排期与学费：报名前请向 TTW 确认。

From an idea to a part you designed. Start with shapes and dimensions, then explore fit, function and iteration.

Learners move from a sketch to a digital model using beginner-friendly CAD tools, then take that design through slicing and printing.
Learning outcomes: sketch an idea into a simple 3D digital model; use measurements/scale to design a part that fits a real object; prepare a model for printing (basic slicing settings); compare a printed prototype with its intended fit; revise a design through more than one iteration.

**Entry note**: Introductory design and advanced CAD need different starting points; share the learner's grade/experience.

## DTF Printing (`dtf-printing`)
**Grades 7–9 · High School · Adult** — Future pathway · Register interest. Schedule confirmed (see schedule.md); no fixed per-class price published for this program specifically.
中文：DTF 转印（7–9 年级 · 高中 · 成人）— 未来学习方向·登记意向；已有排期，尚无公布的固定学费。

From a design file to a wearable transfer. Learn how direct-to-film printing turns a digital design into a durable, full-colour transfer pressed onto fabric.

Learning outcomes: prepare a design file (resolution, colour, format); explain the DTF process (printing, powder, curing); press a transfer correctly (temperature/pressure/timing); follow safety steps around heat and equipment; compare a finished press against the original and adjust.

**Entry note**: A hands-on production skill for learners comfortable with safety steps around heat/equipment. Age suitability, group size and equipment access confirmed individually before enrollment. **Using DTF equipment requires completing safety training and the related course first** (see membership.md).
