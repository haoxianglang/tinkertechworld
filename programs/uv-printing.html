UV Printing

Print directly onto a surface, cured by UV light.

Learn how UV printers apply ink directly onto rigid or flexible surfaces—like acrylic, wood or metal—and cure it instantly with UV light. Explore how material, ink layers and finish affect the result.

What learning looks like

Skills with a purpose.

  • Prepare a design file for a specific surface and print area.
  • Understand how UV-cured ink layers, gloss and texture affect a finished piece.
  • Test print settings on sample materials before a final run.

Illustrative project · Not a student result

Print a custom design on a rigid surface

Choose a small object or material sample, prepare a design file sized to fit it, and print a test piece. Compare colour and finish against the original design and adjust.
