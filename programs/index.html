Grades 7–9 · High School

DTF Printing

Future pathway · Register interest

Learn how direct-to-film (DTF) printing turns a digital design into a durable, full-colour transfer that can be pressed onto fabric. Understand the process from file prep to finished press.

Explore this program →

Grades 7–9 · High School

UV Printing

Future pathway · Register interest

Learn how UV printers apply ink directly onto rigid or flexible surfaces—like acrylic, wood or metal—and cure it instantly with UV light. Explore how material, ink layers and finish affect the result.

Explore this program →
