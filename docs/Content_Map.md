## Copy guardrails
Use understandable outcomes: “explain a test”, “design a useful part”, “make a robot more reliable”. Do not repeat unsupported competitor critiques (“camps teach nothing”, “coding is only screen time”), market forecasts, operational assumptions or university-admissions guarantees from strategic brainstorming.

Before changing future status to active, verify actual course name, prerequisite, session count/length, available dates, tuition/tax, inclusions, instructor, venue, supervision and enrollment terms. Change both languages together. Replace illustrative gallery entries with permissioned projects only when real evidence is available.
